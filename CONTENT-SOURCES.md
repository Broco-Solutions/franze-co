# Franze & Co. Content Sources

## Public catalog status — 2026-10-01

Franze has **no published product records or product photography** until each record is backed by an authorized supplier source, image-rights confirmation, and capture date.

The former 24-product catalog and all 60 photographs were removed after a cross-repository audit showed that they were identical to Nuvola Home's catalog. They are not Franze sources and must not be restored.

## Publication gate

Before adding a product or visual asset, document:

- supplier or rights-holder and original URL/file;
- written authorization or licence status for the image;
- source product name, SKU, specifications, and capture date;
- local asset path and SHA-256 hash.

`pnpm audit:nuvola-overlap` is required before publishing.
